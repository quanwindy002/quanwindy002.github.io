Privacy Policy

WindyLand Upload is a private publishing tool used only by Windy Land to schedule and publish its own short videos about the Huế real-estate market to the Windy Land accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • From TikTok, after the account owner signs in with Login Kit: the account's open_id, display name and avatar (user.info.basic), and the creator settings TikTok returns before posting (allowed privacy levels, comment/duet/stitch availability, maximum video length).
  • The videos, captions and cover images that Windy Land itself prepares for upload.

How we use it

  • Only to show who is posting, to let the owner choose the post settings, and to upload and publish the owner's video (video.upload, video.publish).
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, do not use it for advertising, and do not access other users' data.

Storage and security

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own Windows computer, in a private folder, and are never sent anywhere except to the official TikTok, Google and Meta APIs.
  • No server or database run by us keeps TikTok data.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner signs out or revokes access in TikTok (Settings › Security › Manage app permissions). Deleting the local token file removes all stored TikTok data. Requests: quanwindy002@gmail.com.

Terms of Service
